About QR Code Studio

A practical, privacy-conscious QR utility designed for everyday work.

VERSION 1.1.5

QR Code Studio

Generate. Customize. Scan. Share.

QR Code Studio combines QR code creation, visual customization, scanning, local bulk processing, export, and project management in a single HTML application. Core generation and export operations are designed to run locally without an account or server.

Privacy model
Core payloads and local images are processed in the browser. Projects remain in browser storage until exported or deleted.
Browser requirements
Camera scanning depends on secure-context browser APIs and camera permission, and generally requires HTTPS or localhost. Native QR detection is preferred. For broader compatibility, this version embeds the Nimiq JavaScript QR decoder directly in the HTML. It runs locally and does not require first-time downloads or decoder caching.

Current capabilities and limitations

  • Static QR generation uses an embedded QR encoder and supports QR versions 1–40, UTF-8 ECI for non-ASCII content, and error-correction levels L, M, Q, and H.
  • Dynamic QR editing and hosted scan analytics require an external redirect service or backend and are not simulated by this offline tool.
  • Live and image QR decoding use native QR detection when available and can fall back to the bundled Nimiq JavaScript decoder. The decoder is embedded in this HTML and is available offline, subject to browser support for workers and local file handling.
  • PDF export produces a genuine PDF containing a high-quality raster rendering, not editable vector QR paths. For the sharpest scalable artwork, use SVG; verify all outputs at final size before printing.
  • Excel import supports XLSX files when the browser provides compatible ZIP/Deflate decompression; CSV and TSV remain available as dependable alternatives. Imports are limited to 20 MB, 1,000 records, and 100 columns per batch.
  • Arabic selection enables right-to-left layout and translates key navigation and generator headings. Some detailed forms and help content remain in English in this version.

Dependencies and attribution

QR encoding, rendering, ZIP creation, PDF creation, and the Nimiq qr-scanner 1.4.2 legacy decoder are embedded in this HTML file. The bundled decoder is initialized and tested against a locally generated sample QR at startup. No scanner bundle is downloaded at runtime, and QR images or payloads are not sent to a decoder provider. Scanner camera access still requires browser permission and usually HTTPS or localhost. No analytics or advertising integrations are included.
